✦ THE PLAYGROUND

Eight themed zones —
a whole universe made for kids

While you enjoy your coffee, the kids get a whole universe. From the launch pad to the stardust sea, every corner is built for curiosity — safe, spotless and sanitised throughout the day.

🚀 01

Rocket Slides

Climb the giant rocket and swoop down from the top of the planet — squeals and giggles at full launch.

🌟 02

Stardust Ball Pit

Dive into an ocean of thousands of shimmering “stardust” balls — like swimming through the stars.

🛸 03

Soft-Play Galaxy

A soft, safe padded kingdom for climbing, rolling and weaving — the whole galaxy is a playground.

🧱 04

LEGO Planet

A creative base for little master builders — stack up your very own planets and starships, brick by brick.

🪐 05

Obstacle Course

A courage course through the “asteroid belt” — balance, coordination and bravery, one gate at a time.

🕹️ 06

Arcade & Games

Light up the screens — round after round of interactive adventures, waiting for a brand-new high score.

👩‍🚀 07

Dress-Up Zone

Suit up, helmet on — and star in your very own imaginary space drama.

🍼 08

Toddler Bay

A gentle little corner of the cosmos for babies 0–3 — separate, safe and worry-free first adventures.
